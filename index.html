Adventure

Off-Road 4WD Exploring

Rent a Jeep and explore hundreds of miles of red dirt backroads. You can rent one right at the resort's Love Lānaʻi Desk.

Munro Trail

About 7 miles along the high ridge, by foot or mountain bike, with sweeping views across the island to Maui and Molokaʻi. Best when it's been dry.

Lānaʻi Adventure Park

Zip lines, aerial obstacle courses, and guided e-bike or UTV tours.

In town

  • Bring cash for Blue Ginger.
  • Small town hours change, so call ahead before driving up.
  • Lānaʻi City Grill is closed Sunday and Monday.

Island tips

  • Cell service is spotty off the main roads. Download offline maps.
  • Wave to other drivers. Everybody does.
  • Please leave rocks and sites as you find them.
